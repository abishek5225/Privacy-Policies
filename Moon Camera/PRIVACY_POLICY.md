# Privacy Policy for Moon Camera

**Last updated:** November 24, 2026

Moon Camera ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android camera app for photographing and recording the moon and other distant subjects. This policy explains how the app handles information. It describes the app as it works in the version released on the date above.

## 1. Information the app accesses

Moon Camera asks for the permissions below. You can refuse them, or turn them off later, in Android Settings.

- **Camera.** Used to show the live viewfinder and to take photos and record videos. The app cannot take photos without this permission.
- **Microphone.** Used only while you record a video, to record sound with it. Photos never use the microphone. If you do not allow the microphone, videos are recorded without sound.
- **Photos and videos on your device.** Captures are saved to your device's shared gallery, in the Pictures/MoonCamera and Movies/MoonCamera folders. The in-app gallery shows the photos and videos it can access, so you can view, favorite, share or delete them.
- **Motion sensors.** The app reads the device's accelerometer to show the level and steadiness indicators in Telescope mode. These readings are used on your device to draw those indicators.

## 2. How the information is used

Information is used to provide the camera features described above. In the current version, camera, microphone and motion input are processed on your device. Moon Camera does not require an account or sign-in.

## 3. Sharing

Moon Camera passes a photo or video to another app only when you choose Share or Open with. Android then gives the app you picked access to that file, and that app's own privacy policy applies to it.

## 4. Storage and deletion

Photos and videos stay in your gallery until you delete them. Uninstalling Moon Camera does not delete files saved in Pictures/MoonCamera or Movies/MoonCamera. You can delete them from the Moon Camera gallery or from any gallery app.

## 5. Changes planned for future versions

We may add features in later versions that handle information differently from the current version, including:

- **Advertising.** Ads may be shown in the app. Ad providers, such as Google AdMob, may use identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, and information about how you use the app, to show and measure ads. Ad providers handle that information under their own privacy policies.
- **Crash and diagnostic reports.** We may collect crash reports and diagnostics. These can include your device model, Android version, app version and crash stack traces, and they help us fix bugs.

Before we release a version that starts doing either of these, we will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date. Where the law requires it, we will also ask for your consent first, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. We will describe the change in the release notes.

## 6. Children

Moon Camera is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will act on it.

## 7. Security

Photos and videos are stored in your device's shared storage, which Android protects with its permission system. Access to the camera and microphone is controlled by Android, and you can withdraw either permission at any time.

## 8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information that we hold about you, and to object to or restrict some uses of it. To use these rights, contact us at the address above. Information held by future features described in section 5 will be covered by the process we publish when those features are released.
